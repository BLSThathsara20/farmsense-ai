### How to use

1. Open the post `.md` file.
2. Pick **1 title** from the 10 recommended titles (or mix your own).
3. Paste the title as the first bold line / hook, then copy the **LinkedIn post** body.
4. For post 01: upload your landing page screenshot to ChatGPT, paste the **image prompt**, generate a clean square/landscape graphic, download, attach to the post.
5. Post with 3–5 hashtags max (already suggested).

### Tip

Space posts 2–4 days apart so the series feels natural, not spammy.
