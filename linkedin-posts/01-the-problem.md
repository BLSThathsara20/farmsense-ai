## LinkedIn post (copy this)

Most small farmers do not fail because they work less.

They fail because one decision is hard:

**What should I plant this season?**

Then two more questions follow fast:

- When should I sow?
- When should I sell?

I grew up around this kind of pressure.  
People plant what they planted last year.  
Or what a neighbour planted.  
Or what “feels safe.”

That is not laziness.  
That is missing information.

Soil may be good for a crop.  
But the local market can already be full.  
Prices can fall just when everyone harvests.  
Weather can turn against the plan.

So the real problem is not “no advice.”  
The real problem is **advice in pieces**.

Soil in one place.  
Weather in another.  
Prices somewhere else.  
And the farmer still has to join it all in their head.

That gap is why I started building **FarmSense AI** for my final-year Computing project at Northumbria University.

Not another fancy dashboard.  
A clear plan before a single seed goes into the ground:

**what crop · when to sow · when to sell**

This is post 1 of a short series.  
Next, I will share the idea behind the product — and what I am building.

If you work in farming, agritech, or student projects, I would love your thoughts:

**What makes crop planning hardest where you are?**
